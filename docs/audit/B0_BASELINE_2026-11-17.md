## Existing findings — deliberately NOT corrected in B0

1. The published standard deviations for **XGB / v3_2_combatfix** differ from the original aggregation (publication issue, not a discrepancy in the individual predictions).
2. The final RF and XGB benchmark does **not** use an identical fitting budget: RF fits train+validation, XGB uses validation for early stopping.
3. The Deep MLP candidate runner computes test probabilities during candidate exploration; a strict one-shot test protocol is not yet satisfied.
4. The clean-room parser does not generate all the final versioned dataset features; replay-unit accounting and missing winner behavior need specific regression tests.
5. The README, canonical paper paths and reproducibility check scripts require alignment.

These findings are scheduled for B1–B4; their presence in the baseline is expected and must not be hidden.
